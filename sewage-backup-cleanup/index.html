Sewage coming up from a drain, tub or toilet?

Wastewater rising through a floor drain, shower or toilet means the line is blocked or the main is backing up. This page is about the contaminated water left in the house after the backup, which professionals call Category 3 water under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard.

A plumber clears the blockage. A restoration crew removes the contaminated water and anything it soaked, sanitizes the area and dries it. If several neighbors have the same problem, the blockage may be in the street main, and the City of Bartow utility can tell you whether it is. Standing water is first pumped as described on water extraction, and soaked flooring is on carpet water damage.

Sewage backup emergency: stay out and call

A sewage backup is an emergency at any hour, and the 24/7 line is open at night and on weekends. Same-day service is offered when crews are available. Keep children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system out of the area, stop using sinks, showers, toilets and the washer, and do not try to wet-vacuum the water yourself.

Do not run the HVAC system if sewage is in the house, because the air handler can spread contamination. If water is near outlets or the panel, cut power only if the panel is dry to reach, and call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas concern first.

Protect yourself until the crew arrives

  • Wear boots and rubber gloves if you must enter, and wash hands thoroughly after.
  • Keep food, medicine and dishes out of the affected room.
  • Photograph the affected area from a distance without touching anything.
  • Do not use bleach and ammonia together, and do not mix cleaning chemicals.

What gets removed and what gets cleaned

Usually removed

  • Carpet and pad
  • Insulation and soaked drywall
  • Upholstered furniture
  • Cardboard and paper goods

Usually cleaned and kept

  • Concrete and tile
  • Framing and sealed wood
  • Metal and glass items
  • Hard plastics

Porous materials soak up contamination and cannot be reliably sanitized, so they are taken out. Hard surfaces and framing are cleaned with disinfectants and then dried. The EPA advises bringing in a professional who works with contaminated water when sewage is involved.

The cleanup in order

  1. 1

    Contain

    Crews isolate the area and put on gloves, boots, masks and eye protection.

  2. 2

    Extract

    Contaminated water is pumped to the sanitary sewer or contained for disposal.

  3. 3

    Remove and clean

    Affected porous materials are bagged, and hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected.

  4. 4

    Dry and verify

    Dehumidifiers and air movers run until readings return to normal.

If floodwater caused the backup, see flood damage cleanup. For the drying stage, structural drying and dehumidification explains the equipment and monitoring.

Backups in older Bartow neighborhoods

Homes in the older Northeast and South Bartow historic districts may have aging sewer laterals, and tree roots and old clay or cast iron lines are common causes of blockages anywhere. A camera inspection by a plumber finds the cause so the backup does not return.

Ask the City of Bartow, which runs municipal sewer service, which part of the line is the property owner responsibility and which belongs to the city.

Insurance coverage for sewage backups and what drives cost

Many homeowners policies exclude sewer and drain backups unless a specific endorsement was added. Check your policy for a water backup rider and ask your insurer before work begins, and photograph the damage and keep records of every step.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle sewage backup cleanup emergencies at night or on weekends in Bartow?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, and same-day service is offered when crews are available.

Why is sewage water in my house when the toilet is not clogged?

A blockage in the main line or a failed sewer lateral can push waste water up through the lowest drain. A plumber finds the cause.

Can I clean up sewage myself?

Small amounts on hard surfaces can be cleaned with protection, but larger backups, wet carpet and drywall need trained crews.

Does homeowners insurance cover sewage backup?

Many policies exclude it unless a water backup endorsement was purchased, so check your policy.

Is it safe to stay in the house?

Stay out of the affected rooms and keep children, pets and anyone with health concerns away. Ask a physician about health questions.
