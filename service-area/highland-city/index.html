Highland City, the Fort Fraser Trail and Lake Hancock

Highland City is an unincorporated community on US 98, northwest of Bartow, and it takes its name from the 1837 Fort Fraser. The Fort Fraser Trail runs 7.75 miles from SR 60 in Bartow to CR 540 in Lakeland and has a trailhead here, so the trail is how many residents know the area.

The Circle B Bar Reserve sits about 2.5 miles north on Lake Hancock. That matters to a homeowner because a lake that rises with heavy rain is a reason to check flood maps, and because water that reaches a yard is not the same as water from a broken pipe.

When water is spreading in Highland City

A pipe failure, a roof opening or a failed appliance can send water across a floor in minutes, and the 24/7 line is open to Highland City callers on nights and weekends. Same-day service is offered when crews are available, so call when water is moving rather than waiting for morning.

Storm water is the cause most tied to this area. Rain driven through a roof or a window is covered on storm water damage cleanup, and a stained or sagging ceiling that follows is handled on ceiling water damage repair.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Stop the source

Shut the main water valve, or the small valve behind a toilet, washer or sink.

Power with care

Turn off circuits to wet rooms only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without stepping in water.

Lift what you can

Raise rugs, boxes and electronics onto counters or furniture above the waterline.

Keep out of dirty water

Keep children and pets away from water that came from a drain, a toilet or outside.

Utilities and permits for Highland City properties

Water and sewer service in unincorporated Polk County can come from Polk County Utilities or from another provider. Ask whether your account is with the county before you call a utility about a main break. A bill or the meter box lid usually names the provider.

Because Highland City is not a city, a permit for a water heater swap, a re-pipe or structural repair goes to Polk County Building rather than a city building department. Ask the county what applies before a contractor starts, and keep any permit paperwork with your insurance file.

Rain season and the water that follows it

The National Weather Service Tampa Bay office defines the rainy season for West Central Florida as May 25 to October 10, and the hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. Those months are when roofs, attic penetrations and window seals get tested hardest in Highland City.

After a storm, check the ceiling in the rooms under your attic and the walls under any window that faced the wind. A stain that grows after rain is an active leak, and drying it within about 24 to 48 hours, as the EPA recommends, is what keeps it from becoming a mold problem. Dry-out follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration.
