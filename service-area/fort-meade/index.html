River season, rain season and Fort Meade

The Peace River flooded pastures and part of US 98 around Fort Meade during Hurricane Ian in 2022, and the 430-acre Peace River Park sits along the same river. In a wet year the river, not a pipe, is the main flood threat for properties near it.

The National Weather Service Tampa Bay office defines the rainy season for West Central Florida as May 25 to October 10. If your lot is near the river or low ground, check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address, and ask the city what has flooded before.

When water is spreading in Fort Meade

Rising water from the river or from heavy rain is a flood, and the 24/7 line takes Fort Meade flood calls at night and on weekends. Same-day service is offered when crews are available and the site is safe to enter. The cleanup follows flood damage cleanup, because river water is Category 3 from the start.

Water from inside the house, such as a split pipe or a failed water heater, is a different job. A burst line is on burst pipe water damage, and standing water is on water extraction.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

A city utility that has had main breaks

Fort Meade provides its own water and sewer, and the city has issued boil-water notices after water main breaks. When a notice is in effect, do not drink tap water, brush teeth with it or make ice from it until the city lifts the notice.

A main break at the street is the city job, while a break inside your property is a plumber job. If a break flooded a floor, photograph it and call the restoration line. Ask the Fort Meade utility office which side of the meter is yours.

What Fort Meade customers need after the water leaves

Floodwater soaks carpet, drywall and insulation, and those materials usually come out rather than dry in place. Hard surfaces are cleaned and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers, as described on structural drying and dehumidification.

Homeowners policies typically do not cover flood damage, according to FEMA, and a National Flood Insurance Program policy generally takes effect 30 days after purchase. For neighboring places, see Mulberry and Lakeland.
