The streets south of Main Street

The National Register of Historic Places lists the South Bartow Residential District. It covers about 110 acres, roughly bounded by Floral and First Avenues and by Main and Vine Streets, and it was listed in 1993. The listing gives 1885 as the earliest date and names Colonial Revival, Bungalow and Craftsman, and vernacular wood frame houses.

The listing is a boundary on a map, and residents use the name more loosely. Give your street address when you call. Houses just outside the district have their own ages, and the same steps apply to them.

Wood frames, plaster and floors you may want to keep

A wood frame house can take water up through floors and down through ceilings. Floors, subfloors, baseboards and plaster all hold moisture, and a surface that looks dry can still read wet in a meter. The first job is to find the edges of the wet area.

A wet wood floor does not always have to come out. Whether it can be saved depends on how long it was wet, whether the water was clean, and how the boards and finish respond to drying. The steps are on hardwood floor water damage repair, and wet walls are covered on wall and drywall water damage repair.

If your house sits above a crawl space

Some older houses sit on piers or blocks with open space below. If you see water or damp insulation under the floor, do not crawl in alone. See crawl space water damage for what to check from the outside first.

Old houses, new plumbing

The age of a house and the age of its pipes are not the same. Many historic houses have had plumbing replaced in stages, so one bathroom may have new lines while the kitchen still has old ones. A plumber can tell you what material is in your walls.

The usual leak sources in any older house are supply lines, water heaters, washing machine hoses and a clogged air conditioner drain. A pipe that has split is covered on burst pipe water damage.

Permits and the review board before you rebuild

Drying a house and rebuilding it are two steps. Before you rebuild finished exterior features, ask the City of Bartow Planning Department whether the work needs review. The Historic Architectural Review Board looks at changes to contributing properties inside designated historic district boundaries.

Permits for building, electrical, plumbing and mechanical work come from the Building Department at 450 N Wilson Ave. The board meets on the first Thursday of the month when it has items, and the City Clerk can send the agenda.

What we found about flooding here

Plumbing and roofs, not a river record

We found no source reporting river flooding of the South Bartow district. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified, so this page treats leaks, storm rain and appliance failures as the likely causes and does not call the area flood prone.

The Peace River history officials describe in Bartow belongs to East Bartow. For a map flood zone, check your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.

A first-response list for a wet house

Water off first

Close the fixture valve or the main. A slow drip behind a wall is still water.

Lift what is on the floor

Rugs, books and boxes wick water. Put them on a dry surface.

Leave the plaster alone

Do not drill or break into old plaster walls to look. Moisture readings find the wet area without opening it.

Write down the timeline

Note when you found the leak and what you did. Photos with dates help later.

Documentation for a historic house claim

Coverage depends on the cause and on your policy. Report the loss to your insurer and ask what it needs. Photos, dated notes and moisture readings help show what was wet and what was dried. Your insurer, not us, decides coverage.

FAQ

South Bartow water damage questions

Can an old wood floor be dried and kept?

Sometimes. It depends on how long it was wet and whether the water was clean. Readings during drying show how the boards respond, and a replacement decision comes after that.

Are old plaster walls handled differently from drywall?

They dry differently. Plaster is dense and can hold water longer, so the area is measured before anything is cut. The wet edges are found first.

Will historic review slow emergency drying?

Emergency extraction and drying are not exterior changes. Review applies when you alter a contributing property, so ask the city before rebuilding outside features.

My house is on piers and the floor feels damp. What now?

Check from outside for standing water and call. Do not crawl under the house yourself, because wet crawl spaces can hide electrical and structural hazards.

Is South Bartow in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Zones are set by address, so check yours on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center.
