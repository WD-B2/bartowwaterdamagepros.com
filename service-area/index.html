Neighborhoods in Bartow

Three Bartow neighborhoods have their own pages: Downtown Bartow, South Bartow and East Bartow. Neighborhood names are used loosely, so give your street address when you call.

Bartow and the 33830 and 33831 ZIP codes

Bartow is the Polk County seat, with ZIP codes 33830 and 33831.

The City of Bartow owns its water, sewer and electric utilities. Its Building Department at 450 N Wilson Ave issues building, electrical, plumbing and mechanical permits, and Polk County Building at 330 W Church St serves the unincorporated areas. See all services for the full list.

East Bartow has a history of flooding from the Peace River. After Hurricane Ian in 2022 the river stayed high for weeks, and in August 2024 Tropical Storm Debby brought the river to 7.6 feet and a local emergency declaration. Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address. Dry-out work follows the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration.

Local notes by town

Highland City

An unincorporated community on US 98 northwest of Bartow, near the Fort Fraser Trail and Lake Hancock. Permits go through Polk County Building, and storm and ceiling leaks are the usual calls. See Highland City.

Eagle Lake

A city on the US 17 corridor north of Bartow, with its own water and sewer systems. It offers a sewer credit for water service leaks if you ask within 30 days. See Eagle Lake.

Mulberry

A city incorporated in 1901 with phosphate heritage, where the city runs water, sewer, sanitation and stormwater. Older pipes explain many calls. See Mulberry.

Fort Meade

A city south of Bartow near the Peace River, which flooded pastures and part of US 98 during Hurricane Ian in 2022. The city runs its own water, sewer and electric utility. See Fort Meade.

Lakeland

A large city northwest of Bartow with a lake chain that took a record rain during Hurricane Milton in 2024. Its utilities are city-owned, and four ZIP codes are covered. See Lakeland.

How to pick the right town page

Start with the town where the damaged building sits, not where you receive mail. Some addresses carry a Bartow mailing address while sitting in unincorporated Polk County, and that changes which office issues permits and which utility serves the property.

If you are not sure, look at your water bill for the provider name, or ask the Polk County Building office at 330 W Church St which jurisdiction applies. Then choose the page that matches. Each one explains the local utility contacts, the weather history that matters and the ZIP codes served.

Towns without their own page, such as Homeland, Eaton Park, Lakeshore, Nichols, Alturas and Bradley, are served by the same crews. Use the closest town page for local conditions, and mention the community when you call so dispatch can plan the route.
