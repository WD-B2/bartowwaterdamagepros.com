A city utility and a leak credit worth knowing about

The City of Eagle Lake provides water and sewer service through its Eagle Lake and Green Acres systems, and it has a Utility Department and a Building Department. A main break at the street or a permit question starts there.

The city offers a sewer credit for water service leaks and pool fills. A customer has to request it within 30 days and bring the plumber receipts and photos, and the credit adjusts the sewer charge but not the water usage. If a hidden leak ran up your bill, ask the Utility Department about the credit early and keep every receipt from the repair.

Eagle Lake, the three lakes and the US 17 corridor

Eagle Lake began as a settlement in 1880, when John Bingham settled on a knoll between Eagle Lake, Crystal Lake and Spirit Lake. The city was incorporated in 1921, and most of it follows a stretch of US 17 north of Bartow, where the road also meets CR 540.

Homes near any of the lakes are a reason to check the FEMA flood map for the address and to ask the city what has flooded before. Rising lake or surface water is a flood, and that cleanup follows flood damage cleanup rather than a plumbing repair.

Can I get a credit on my sewer bill after a water leak?

The City of Eagle Lake offers a sewer credit for water service leaks and pool fills. The request must be made within 30 days with plumber receipts and photos, and water usage itself is not adjusted. Ask the Utility Department for the current rules.
