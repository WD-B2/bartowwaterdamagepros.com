The record rain and what it did to Lakeland lakes

Hurricane Milton, October 2024

The City of Lakeland reported more than 12 inches of rain in 24 hours during Hurricane Milton, and relayed that the National Weather Service called the rainfall a 1-in-1,000-year event. Lake Bonny, Lake Parker and Lake Mirror flooded, and homes near them took water.

About 2 million gallons of partly treated wastewater also spilled into the stormwater system that feeds Lake Somerset. The lakes in that chain drain toward Lake Hancock and the Peace River, which is why a Lakeland storm can matter for Bartow properties downstream.

Practical takeaway: a home near one of these lakes should have its FEMA flood map zone checked, and a flood policy takes about 30 days to take effect, according to FEMA. Check your coverage before the next storm rather than during it.

A city-owned utility and a building inspection office

Lakeland owns its electric utility, Lakeland Electric, and provides city water and wastewater service. If a storm takes out power while water is in the house, the utility is the first call for any electrical hazard, and then the restoration line.

The city Building Inspection Division handles permits. Ask whether a roof repair, a water heater swap or a re-pipe needs a permit, and keep the paperwork with your insurance file.
