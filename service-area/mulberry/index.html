A city incorporated in 1901 and what that means for pipes

Mulberry was incorporated in 1901, and its history is tied to phosphate mining. A city that old has a mix of early-twentieth-century houses and newer builds, and the older ones are more likely to have original supply lines, cast iron drains and aging sewer laterals.

That affects what fails. Old galvanized supply pipe corrodes from the inside and can split at a joint, and clay or cast iron drain lines let roots in, which brings sewage backups. A backup is covered on sewage backup cleanup, and a split supply line on burst pipe water damage.

Who to ask in Mulberry: the city or the county

Inside the city limits, water, sewer and stormwater are city services, so a main break or a sewer problem at the street starts with the City of Mulberry. A pipe problem inside your walls is a plumber job, and the cleanup afterward is ours.

If the blockage affects several houses on a street, the city main may be the cause, and the city can check it. Photograph anything that came into the house before it is cleaned, and keep the photos with your insurance claim.

Storm season in a stormwater-run city

The rainy season in West Central Florida runs May 25 to October 10, according to the National Weather Service Tampa Bay office, and drains and ditches work hardest in those months. When street water backs toward a garage or a low door, that is surface flooding and follows flood damage cleanup.

Mulberry water damage questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Mulberry?

Yes. Mulberry uses ZIP code 33860, which also covers Bradley Junction, Fuller Heights and Willow Oak along SR 60 and SR 37, and neighboring Bradley uses 33835. Both are served from the Bartow dispatch.

Is emergency water removal available in Mulberry?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays, and same-day service is offered when crews are available.

Does the city handle sewage backups in my house?

The City of Mulberry runs the municipal sewer, so a blockage in the city main is the city responsibility. A blockage inside your own line is a plumber job, and the cleanup of contaminated water is a restoration job.

Why do older Mulberry homes have more plumbing leaks?

Older galvanized supply lines corrode from inside, and old drain lines can crack or let roots in. A camera inspection by a plumber finds the cause.
