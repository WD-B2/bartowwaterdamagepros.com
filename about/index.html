Who we are

We are Bartow Water Damage Pros, a water damage restoration service for homes and businesses in Bartow, Highland City, Eagle Lake, Mulberry, Fort Meade and Lakeland.

Bartow is the county seat, with the Old Polk County Courthouse (1909) downtown and historic residential districts that date to the 1880s and early 1900s. Older homes and a wet climate are the reason water damage work is steady here. Our job is to answer the phone when it happens and to explain what comes next in plain words.

What we handle

The work falls into three groups. Emergency calls cover water that is still moving or has just stopped, such as burst pipes, floods and sewage backups. Drying work covers structural drying and specific materials like hardwood and carpet. Specialty work covers mold, which follows moisture and is kept separate from drying.

Every service has its own page. See all services to find the one that matches your situation, or call and describe it.

How a job works

  1. 1

    Call

    Describe what happened and where the water is. The line is open 24/7.

  2. 2

    Scope

    The crew finds the source, checks how far the water traveled and explains scope and price before work starts.

  3. 3

    Remove and dry

    Standing water comes out, wet materials are removed where needed, and the structure is dried until readings are normal.

  4. 4

    Document

    Photos and moisture readings are kept for your insurance claim.

Standards we follow

Water damage work follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, and mold work follows the IICRC S520 standard. These are industry standards. They guide how water is classified and how drying is verified.

Mold assessment and remediation in Florida are licensed by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation. An assessor may not remediate a structure they assessed within the previous 12 months, so testing and cleanup are kept separate. See mold remediation for details.

Insurance and paperwork

We document damage with photos and moisture readings, and we keep the records so you have them for your claim. We do not decide coverage. Your insurer does.

The Florida Department of Financial Services advises photographing damage before temporary repairs and contacting your insurer before you sign a repair contract. We follow the same advice when we talk with customers. Florida has also banned assignment of benefits for property policies issued or renewed on or after January 1, 2023, so you file the claim and the contractor is paid under the terms you agree to.

What we do not do

Fix the plumbing

A plumber repairs the pipe, fixture or line. We deal with the water it released.

Promise a price by phone

No exact price can be given from a description alone. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

Decide your claim

Coverage belongs to your insurer and your policy.

Skip the readings

Drying is verified by moisture readings, not by a calendar.

Knowing the area

The City of Bartow owns its water, sewer and electric utilities. Its Building Department at 450 N Wilson Ave issues building, electrical, plumbing and mechanical permits, and Polk County Building at 330 W Church St serves the unincorporated areas around the city.

East Bartow has a history of flooding from the Peace River, and the lakes to the north rose during recent storms. Those facts change how a job is approached. A pipe leak and a flood are different problems, and the local pages explain the difference for Highland City, Eagle Lake, Mulberry, Fort Meade and Lakeland.

Questions people ask us first

Is this an emergency?

If water is still moving, or has soaked floors, walls or a ceiling, call now. Water left in place keeps causing damage.

Who pays?

Your insurer decides what your policy covers. We explain scope and price before work starts so you can make that call with real numbers.

How long will it take?

It depends on what was wet. Drying is finished when moisture readings are normal, not on a set day.

Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to give the crew access. Tell the dispatcher about locks, gates and pets on the call.
