Frozen pipe emergency: water appears when the ice melts

A pipe that froze solid can already be split, and the leak only starts when the ice turns back to water. That is why frozen pipe floods often begin on a warmer afternoon after a cold night. The line is open 24/7, so call at once if water is coming from a wall, ceiling or crawl space, and ask about same-day service.

Close the main water valve first. If a ceiling is sagging or bulging with water, stay out from under it and keep the room clear. Never use an open flame, torch or heat gun on a frozen pipe, because it can start a fire inside a wall. A split line that has wet a wall is covered on wall and drywall repair, and the first pumping is described on water extraction.

How common is this in Bartow?

Hard freezes are not routine in Polk County, so most Bartow homes are not built or wrapped for them. That is the real risk: attic supply lines, exposed pipes on outside walls, pool equipment and irrigation backflow assemblies are rarely insulated, so a single cold snap can split them.

This page is about the mechanism and the cleanup, not a promise that freezes are frequent. When one does happen, plumbers and restoration crews get many calls at once, which is why a quick call helps.

Thawing safely and the standard that follows

  • Shut the main valve before you try to thaw anything.
  • Open a nearby faucet so melting water has somewhere to go.
  • Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer or warm towels, never an open flame.
  • Leave a split pipe alone and call a plumber.

Water released by a split pipe is dried following the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration.

Where freeze damage hides

Attic supply lines

Lines run through unconditioned attic space can split above a ceiling, and the first sign is a stain or sag below.

Outside wall plumbing

Pipes in exterior walls of older Bartow houses can split behind the finished wall without a visible leak for hours.

Exposed outdoor plumbing

Hose bibs, irrigation lines and pool equipment connections take the first hit when temperatures fall.

Garage and utility rooms

Water heater and washer connections in unheated garages are common failure points.

What the cleanup covers once the pipe is repaired

Crews extract standing water, then check ceilings, wall cavities and floors with moisture meters and a thermal camera. Attic insulation soaked from above often has to come out, and a ceiling holding water is opened carefully to drain it before it falls.

The damaged ceiling is covered in detail on ceiling water damage repair, and the general drying steps are on the structural drying page. A licensed plumber has to replace the split section, and the City of Bartow Building Department can tell you whether the repair needs a permit.

What to do and what to avoid after a freeze

Avoid

  • Using a torch or heat gun on a pipe
  • Leaving the main valve open to see whether it leaks
  • Standing under a ceiling that holds water
  • Running electronics near wet floors

Do

  • Shut off the main valve
  • Photograph the split pipe and the damage
  • Keep rooms with wet ceilings empty
  • Call a plumber and the restoration line

Insurers generally look at whether a loss was sudden and whether the owner took reasonable steps to limit it. Photographs, receipts for emergency repairs and a record of when you shut off the water all help. Check your policy for freeze wording and ask your insurer before signing a repair contract.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle frozen pipe water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Bartow?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls at night and on weekends, and same-day service is offered when crews are available.

Can a pipe freeze in Bartow?

A hard freeze can reach Polk County on rare cold nights, and uninsulated pipes in attics, garages and outside walls are the ones that split. When it happens, the water usually shows up at thaw.

Is it safe to thaw the pipe myself?

Only with gentle indirect heat such as a hair dryer, and only if the pipe has not already split. Never use a flame or heat gun, and shut off the main valve if you see any water.

Will my ceiling need to come down?

Only the section that is soaked or sagging. Crews open a small hole to drain trapped water first, then remove only what will not dry.

Do you replace the broken pipe?

No. A licensed plumber does the pipe repair, and the restoration crew handles water removal and drying.
