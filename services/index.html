How we choose the right service for your call

Most calls start with a plain description: a pipe split under the sink, a ceiling that is dripping, a floor that is soft. The person on the line asks where the water came from, how long it has been there and what it touched. That answer decides which page applies and what crews bring.

Clean water from a supply line is handled differently from water that came through a drain or from outside. If you are not sure, call the 24/7 line at (844) 950-6900 and we will help sort it out. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

What to do before we arrive, whatever the cause

  • Stop the water if you can do it safely.
  • Keep out of water that came from a drain or from outside.
  • Photograph everything before it is moved.
  • Call (844) 950-6900 and describe what you see.
