Water damage restoration

Water damage restoration for Bartow homes and businesses

Water damage restoration is the work between the moment water gets in and the moment your building is dry, clean and ready for repairs. It starts with stopping or isolating the source, then extracting standing water, removing materials that cannot be saved and drying the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers until moisture readings return to normal.

We take emergency calls for Bartow, ZIP codes 33830 and 33831, and dispatch around the clock. The EPA says wet materials should be dried within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth, and keeping indoor humidity below 60 percent, ideally between 30 and 50 percent, helps. That timeline is the reason the line is open at night and on weekends.

What to do before we arrive

  • Close the main water valve, or the fixture valve if you know the source.
  • Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  • Lift valuables and electronics off the floor.
  • Keep people and pets out of water from a drain, a toilet or outside.
  • Photograph the damage, then call (844) 950-6900.

The work that follows is guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, which covers how water is classified, how materials are removed or dried and how drying is verified.

Insurance claims and documentation

The Florida Department of Financial Services says a claim should be reported within one year of the loss for an initial or reopened claim, and within 18 months for a supplemental claim. Photograph the damage before any temporary repairs, and contact your insurer before signing a repair contract.

For a large loss, ask your adjuster what documentation is needed. We keep photos and moisture readings through the job so you have them for the claim.

Local conditions

Water, weather and the Peace River around Bartow

The National Weather Service Tampa Bay office defines the West Central Florida rainy season as May 25 to October 10, and hurricane season runs June 1 to November 30. A foot of moving water carries a small car, and six inches can knock over an adult, so avoid driving or wading through floodwater.

East Bartow and the Peace River

East Bartow has flooded from the Peace River before. After Hurricane Ian in 2022 the river stayed high for weeks, and in August 2024 Tropical Storm Debby brought it to 7.6 feet, which prompted an emergency declaration. In October 2024 Hurricane Milton pushed Lake Hancock to emergency levels.

Check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address. A National Flood Insurance Program policy generally takes effect 30 days after purchase, and homeowners policies do not typically cover flood. River and surface floodwater is handled on flood damage restoration.

FAQ

Water damage questions in Bartow

How fast can you get to a water damage emergency in Bartow?

Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the line is open 24/7. Call and describe the situation, and dispatch tells you what to expect.

What should I do first when water is spreading?

Close the main water valve if the source is a pipe, turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry, lift valuables off the floor and photograph the damage. Then call the line.

Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?

It depends on the cause and the policy. Sudden pipe and appliance failures are often covered, while flood usually is not. Contact your insurer before signing a repair contract, and keep photos and moisture readings.

How long does it take for a building to dry?

It depends on the materials and the amount of water, and a crew uses moisture readings rather than a fixed number of days. The EPA advises drying within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold.

What is a large-loss claim and what documentation helps?

A large loss is a claim that affects many rooms or a major part of a building. Ask your adjuster what is required, and keep dated photos, moisture readings, equipment logs and receipts together.

Do you serve areas outside Bartow?

Yes. We serve Highland City, Eagle Lake, Mulberry, Fort Meade and Lakeland, and calls from places like Haines City, Plant City, Winter Haven and Lake Wales are welcome. See the service area page for local details.
