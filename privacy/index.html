Privacy Policy

What this policy covers

This policy describes how Bartow Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website or call our line at (844) 950-6900.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We use the number you call from and what you tell us to respond to your request and to improve our service.

Information we collect

This website does not have forms, so it does not collect information you type in. Like most websites, our host may log technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. We use that information to keep the site running and to understand how it is used.

How we use and share information

We use call details to answer your request and to arrange service. We may share them with the crews who perform the work and with service providers who help us run the line. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic analytics to count visits. You can limit cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

To ask about, correct or delete information we hold about you, email contact@bartowwaterdamagepros.com or call (844) 950-6900.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
