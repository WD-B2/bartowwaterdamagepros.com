Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in Bartow, FL

A kitchen holds more water sources than most rooms, and its cabinets, subfloor and toe-kicks trap moisture. This page covers the cabinet, sink and counter area.

Why kitchen water damage is hard to see

Kitchens have a sink, a dishwasher, a refrigerator line and often a gas or electric range, and almost all of the plumbing sits inside cabinets or behind appliances. A drip under the sink can run for months before anyone sees more than a swollen cabinet floor.

The most common signs are a musty smell under the sink, a cabinet base that has swelled or stained, peeling vinyl or laminate near the dishwasher, and flooring that has begun to lift. If the leak came from a failed appliance, see appliance leak water damage. Water that reached the walls is on wall and drywall repair, and drying is covered on structural drying.

Cabinets, countertops and the subfloor

Particleboard cabinets

Many kitchen cabinets use particleboard that swells and crumbles when wet, so a soaked cabinet base often has to be replaced.

Solid wood and plywood

These materials can often be dried and kept if they have not been wet for long.

Countertops

Stone and laminate tops usually survive, but the substrate under laminate can swell.

Subfloor and toe-kicks

Water collects under cabinets and soaks the subfloor, where it can sit unseen.

What to do first in a wet kitchen

  • Shut the valve under the sink, or the main valve if the source is unknown.
  • Turn off the dishwasher and unplug small appliances if the outlet is dry.
  • Empty the cabinets below the leak and set the items on a dry surface.
  • Photograph the cabinets, the floor and the wall before cleanup.

The work that follows is guided by the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, including whether cabinets can be dried in place or need to be opened.

Typical kitchen scenarios and what happens next

A supply line under the sink splits, and water runs into the cabinet and onto the floor. The crew extracts the water, removes any soaked cabinet base, dries the subfloor and checks the wall behind it.

A dishwasher drain hose loosens and leaks onto the floor for weeks. The crew checks the flooring and subfloor, because long-term leaks are the ones that quietly damage structure.

A leak from an upstairs bathroom comes through the kitchen ceiling. In that case the ceiling is the first priority, and ceiling water damage repair explains the steps.

How a kitchen is dried and repaired

  1. 1

    Extract and inspect

    Water is removed, and moisture meters check cabinets, walls and subfloor.

  2. 2

    Remove wet material

    Toe-kicks, cabinet bases and flooring that cannot dry are taken out.

  3. 3

    Dry

    Air movers and a dehumidifier run, often with ports to dry inside cabinet cavities.

  4. 4

    Rebuild

    Cabinets, flooring and backsplash are replaced after readings are dry.

Kitchen drying often requires removing the toe-kick so air can reach the space under the cabinets. The EPA advises drying water-damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours, so prompt action helps keep cabinets from being a total loss.

Sink and plumbing repairs are separate

A plumber fixes the failed line, trap or shut-off valve. The City of Bartow Building Department at 450 N Wilson Ave issues plumbing permits, so ask whether the repair needs one. Take photographs of the damage and the failed part before anything is thrown away.

Sudden pipe or hose failures are often covered under homeowners policies, while a slow drip ignored for months often is not. Ask your insurer what documents it requires.

FAQ

Common questions

Can wet kitchen cabinets be saved?

Often if they are solid wood or plywood and dried quickly. Particleboard that has swollen usually has to be replaced.

Do you remove the toe-kick?

Yes, when needed, because it gives air access to the space beneath the cabinets and lets crews check for hidden moisture.

Will the kitchen smell go away?

If the water source is fixed and materials are dried, the odor usually fades. Persistent smells can indicate hidden moisture or mold.

Is a dishwasher leak covered by insurance?

Sudden leaks are often covered, while long-term leaks often are not. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

Who repairs the sink plumbing?

A licensed plumber does the plumbing repair, and the restoration crew handles drying and damage repair.
