Is water coming up through the floor or is your water bill too high?

Most people do not search for a slab leak. They search for a warm spot on the tile, a floor that is damp for no reason, the sound of running water when every fixture is off, or a water bill that jumped. Those are the signs of a pressurized line leaking under a concrete slab, which is what a slab leak is.

A simple check helps: turn off every faucet and appliance, then look at the water meter. If the leak indicator or the dial keeps moving, water is escaping somewhere, and a plumber can narrow it down with listening equipment. Inside the city limits the meter is a City of Bartow utility meter. Wet flooring afterward is covered on carpet water damage, and standing water on water extraction.

Slab leak emergency: what to do first

Water coming up through a floor is an emergency, and the 24/7 line takes slab leak calls at night and on weekends. Same-day service is offered when crews are available. Close the main water valve, and if the floor is wet near outlets or the panel, do not stand in the water. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical concern.

A slab leak cleanup is the water damage half of the job. A licensed plumber finds and repairs the leak, whether by breaking into the slab, rerouting the line or replacing it, and the restoration crew dries what the water reached.

Verification and standards for slab leaks

Water that comes up through a slab is dried following the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, with readings in flooring and walls tracked until they return to normal. A plumber locates and repairs the line, and the drying plan waits for that repair, since a running leak keeps the floor wet.

Until then, shut off the water if the meter keeps moving, and avoid standing on warm or wet spots near electrical outlets.

How a slab leak damages a house

Flooring

Water travels along the underside of the floor covering. Tile can pop, laminate cups and carpet pad stays wet.

Baseboards and lower walls

Water wicks up drywall behind the baseboard and stays hidden until paint bubbles.

Hot-water lines

A leaking hot line can warm a section of floor and raise humidity inside the house.

Soil and slab

Water can erode soil under the slab, which is a structural question for an engineer.

Drying after a slab leak

Crews find the extent of moisture with meters and a thermal camera, then lift wet flooring and pad. Drying a floor over concrete uses air movers and dehumidifiers, and sometimes specialty mats or panels that pull moisture up through the surface.

The EPA says to dry water-damaged materials within 24 to 48 hours, and a long-running slab leak has often exceeded that before it is found. That is why moisture mapping, not just surface drying, is the heart of this job.

Where to go for the plumbing side

Ask your plumber about leak detection, spot repair and repiping options, and ask the City of Bartow Building Department whether a plumbing permit applies. Slab work is plumbing work, so a restoration company should not quote it.

For the general drying process see structural drying and dehumidification, and for water that has soaked a finished floor see hardwood floor water damage repair.

Insurance, cost and what to document

Insurance policies treat slab leaks in different ways, and some exclude the cost of reaching the pipe while still covering the water damage. Ask your insurer in plain terms what it covers, and photograph the damaged flooring and the meter reading.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle slab leak water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Bartow?

Yes. The line is open 24/7, and same-day service is offered when crews are available.

Why is there a wet spot on my floor but no visible pipe?

A pressurized line under the slab can push water up through cracks and joints. A plumber confirms the source, and the restoration crew dries the damage.

How do I tell whether the water bill spike is a slab leak?

Turn off all water and look at the meter. If it keeps moving, there is a leak somewhere, and a plumber can narrow it to the slab or another line.

Do you fix the pipe under the slab?

No. A licensed plumber repairs or reroutes the line, and the restoration crew handles drying and cleanup.

Does a slab leak cause mold?

It can if floors and walls stay wet. The EPA recommends drying within 24 to 48 hours, and a leak that has run for weeks needs moisture checks for hidden damage.
